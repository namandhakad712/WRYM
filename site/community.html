Lifecycle · six gates from idea to canon

How a submission travels

stage 1 · you
⚒ Forge
Paint the level, certify it in-browser with the real engine, playtest it, fill the contributor card.
stage 2 · you
⇧ Pull request
One click opens a PR with the level, its certificate and your contributor card embedded.
stage 3 · the bot
🪱 Certification
The bot re-runs the exhaustive validator + policy checks and posts a structured review card with labels.
on failure
↩️ Returned
Failed submissions are returned with exact errors and fix hints. Push a fix — the bot re-certifies automatically.
stage 4 · maintainer
⚖️ Approval
A maintainer judges the design: /wyrm approve merges it, /wyrm request-changes returns it with notes.
stage 5 · canon
🎉 Live
Merged levels join the public ladder within minutes — replays, difficulty profile, your name on the certificate.

The framework · what each side checks

Division of judgement

🪱 wyrm-bot — the machine half deterministic
  • schema: community-2 envelope — name, slug, grid, turn limit, contributor card (name, GitHub handle, design intent, tags)
  • structure: rectangular grid, one head, one heart, known characters, size & budget limits
  • solvability — exhaustive BFS, the same engine the Forge runs
  • certificate honesty: claimed difficulty vs computed (site always shows the computed value)
  • search-space ceiling so CI stays fast; regression re-validation of the whole community ladder
  • slug collisions with the official ladder
⚖️ maintainers — the human half judgement
  • Design quality — is the idea interesting, or a corridor with apples?
  • Fairness — hard but honest: no pixel-perfect luck, no unreadable ambiguity
  • Originality — does it use the vocabulary (valves, ice, splits, bridges) in a new way?
  • Difficulty placement — does the tier fit the ladder's curve?
  • Credit & intent — is the contributor card honest and the intent legible?

commands: /wyrm approve · /wyrm request-changes <reason> · /wyrm revalidate · /wyrm help — maintainers are listed in MAINTAINERS

Label language — the PR state machine

community-level touches the community ladder · bot:validated every machine check passed · bot:awaiting-review certified, waiting for a maintainer · bot:needs-revision returned to the contributor · bot:approved maintainer approved, merge cleared · bot:changes-requested maintainer returned it with notes

Contribute

The bar is deliberately professional: a level must be certified by mathematics and approved by a human before it can shape the leaderboard. That protects every model ranking on this site from a broken or gamed level. Build yours in the Level Forge — the whole flow, from first paint to merged canon, is one uninterrupted pipeline, and every step on this board is public record. The full governance rules live in docs/COMMUNITY_PIPELINE.md.
