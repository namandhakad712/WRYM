Reading the board

The worm — salmon body, coral head + saddle, one googly eye — grows by one segment per apple and may split, leaving a sleeping half behind that still carries weight. Dirt is dug through at the cost of a turn. Walnuts push sokoban-style and park on yellow floor buttons; a golden gate stays barred while any button stands unweighted, then becomes a glowing portal. One-way valves only forgive travel with the arrow. Ice slides the worm until something stops it — one action, many cells. Bridges crumble the instant the last weight leaves them. Keys open padlocks permanently. Spikes kill on contact, even mid-slide. The heart — the pink goal, sparkling — opens only when every apple is eaten.
